Following are the different set of workouts we offers

1) Strength - Our Strength sessions focus on resistance training, bodyweight exercises, and progressive overload to sculpt lean muscle and boost your metabolism. Ideal for anyone looking to get stronger, fitter, and more defined.

2) HRX - Inspired by Bollywood superstar Hrithik Roshan, HRX is a high-energy fusion of functional training and combat moves. Burn calories, challenge your limits, and walk out feeling unstoppable.

3) Dance - Dance your way to fitness with dynamic, choreographed routines set to upbeat music. It’s a full-body cardio workout disguised as pure fun—perfect for all levels and ages!.

4) Burn - Short on time? Our Burn sessions are HIIT-style workouts designed to ignite fat loss and keep your heart pumping. Get maximum results in minimum time with high-impact, full-body exercises.

5) Yoga - Reconnect with yourself through calming flows and mindful movements. Our yoga classes improve flexibility, relieve stress, and bring inner peace—whether you’re a beginner or an advanced yogi.

6) Boxing Bag - Strike, jab, and sweat it out! Our Boxing Bag workouts combine boxing drills with intense cardio, building strength, coordination, and endurance. No opponent—just you versus the bag.
